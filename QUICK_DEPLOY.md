# 🚀 Quick Deploy - Get a Shareable Link in 5 Minutes

## What You Need
- Your GitHub repository (already done ✅)
- A Vercel account (free)

## Steps to Get a Shareable Link

### 1. Sign Up for Vercel
- Go to: https://vercel.com/signup
- Click "Continue with GitHub"
- Authorize Vercel to access your repositories

### 2. Deploy AI Clinical Co-Pilot
- Click "Add New Project"
- Find and select: `healthcare-llm-applications`
- **Important**: Set Root Directory to `AI Clinical Co-Pilot`
- Click "Deploy"
- Wait 2 minutes ⏱️

### 3. Get Your Link
After deployment, Vercel gives you a URL like:
```
https://healthcare-llm-applications.vercel.app
```

### 4. Share This Link
Send this URL to your friend - they can open it on ANY computer!
